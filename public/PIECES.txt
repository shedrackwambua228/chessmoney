Chess piece artwork

The black knight (knight-b.svg) is the original user-supplied SVG, preserved unchanged, with its embedded Uray M. Janos attribution and Creative Commons notice.

The other eleven files replaced failed HTTP downloads with unmodified cburnett SVGs by Colin M. L. Burnett, from the Lichess source repository:
https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/tree/master/public/piece/cburnett

Source filenames map w/b + K,Q,R,B,N,P to king,queen,rook,bishop,knight,pawn-w/b.svg.
